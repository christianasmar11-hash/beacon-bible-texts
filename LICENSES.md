# Licenses and required attribution

These files are public-domain or openly licensed Bible texts assembled for personal use in Beacon Bible. They are hosted as static JSON so the app can look up verses. This is not an official publication of any Bible society.

Do not add copyrighted translations (NIV, CSB, NLT, ESV, NKJV, RVR1960, NVI, and similar) to this collection.

## Public domain

Most texts in this collection are in the public domain. Attribution is not legally required, but the source edition is named in `index.json`.

Special notes:

- **King James Version:** Public domain outside the United Kingdom. In the UK, Crown letters patent still restrict printing.
- **World English Bible / World Messianic Bible:** Public domain. “World English Bible” is a trademark of eBible.org. If the text is changed, do not call the result the World English Bible.
- **Berean Standard Bible and Majority Standard Bible:** Dedicated to the public domain on April 30, 2023. Attribution is appreciated but not required: “The Holy Bible, Berean Standard Bible, BSB is produced in cooperation with Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation Committee.”
- **Reina-Valera 1865:** The 1865 text is public domain. The 2018 orthographic update is CC0 1.0 (Ministerios Valera 1865).
- **Douay-Rheims 1899, the Catholic Public Domain Version, the Clementine Vulgate, and the Russian Synodal translation** use Vulgate or Septuagint Psalm numbering. The shepherd psalm is Psalm 22:1 in those editions, not 23:1.
- **Updated King James Version (UKJV):** Public domain. The author requested this credit line: “I love Jesus (UKJV).”
- **American King James Version (AKJV):** Dedicated to the public domain by Michael Engelbrite on November 8, 1999.
- **New Heart English Bible / Messianic Edition:** Dedicated to the public domain by Wayne A. Mitchell. The structured text here is the CrossWire/scrollmapper edition.
- **Julia E. Smith Translation (1876), Rotherham Emphasized Bible (1902), A Conservative Version, Restored Name King James Version, and the Catholic Public Domain Version** are in the public domain.

Sources include eBible.org, Berean.bible, Ministerios Valera 1865, the Bible SuperSearch public-domain export (Bishops’ Bible and Coverdale), scrollmapper/bible_databases, CrossWire SWORD modules, sacredbible.org, clove3am/julia-smith---bible, and unfoldingWord. This update also adds public-domain editions from eBible.org including Ostervald, the Londona Biblio, Baibala Hemolele 1868, the Karadžić–Daničić Serbian Bible, the Thai Freedom Bible, the Modern Hebrew Bible, the Persian Old Translation, and the Judson Burmese Bible.

## File layout

- `index.json` — catalog of every translation
- `books.json` — 66-book Protestant canon, English and Spanish names, and common abbreviations
- `<CODE>/<book_number>.json` — one book, shaped `{ "book": "John", "chapters": { "1": { "1": "text", ... } } }`
- Book 43 is John; book 45 is Romans; book 19 is Psalms
